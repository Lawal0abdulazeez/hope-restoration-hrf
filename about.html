Who We Are

Hope Restoration and Health Relief Foundation

We are a healthcare-focused non-profit organisation based in Ogun State, Abeokuta, Nigeria. Our work centres on supporting patients and vulnerable individuals with the practical and emotional help they need to live with dignity and independence.

From assisting with personal care and daily living activities to providing medication reminders, health monitoring, and genuine companionship, our team walks alongside those who need us most, always guided by safeguarding principles and respect for every person.

The Foundation was shaped by real experience on the front line of care. Our Manager, Adebayo Elijah, and the wider team bring 6years + of hands-on professional work as Support Workers and Healthcare Assistants, ensuring our programmes remain grounded, practical, and compassionate.

Our Journey

A History of Care

From early community support to a structured foundation serving Ogun State, Abeokuta, and beyond.

2020 - 2021

Roots in Community Support

Informal support networks began forming around vulnerable individuals in Ogun State, Abeokuta, who lacked consistent help with daily living and healthcare follow-up after hospital discharge or during long-term illness.

January 2022

Structured Care Work Begins

Dedicated support work commenced in earnest. Team members provided hands-on assistance with personal hygiene, feeding, dressing, mobility, medication reminders, and accurate care documentation, always prioritising dignity and independence.

2023 - 2024

Growth & Formalisation

The scope of work expanded. Safeguarding protocols, infection-control standards, and structured reporting became central. More families and individuals began receiving consistent companionship and health monitoring support.

2025

Foundation Strengthened

Hope Restoration and Health Relief Foundation consolidated its identity and operations, with clearer programmes focused on community health relief, daily living support, and restoring hope for those who had been overlooked by formal systems.

2026 & Beyond

Continuing the Mission

Today we continue to serve individuals and families across Ogun State, Abeokuta, with the same core values: compassion, professionalism, safeguarding, and a deep belief that every person deserves care that restores hope.
